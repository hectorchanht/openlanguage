What it is呢個係咩

OpenLanguage was not brainstormed — it was lived first. On the all-nighter of 2026-11-19, its founder looped one Japanese song he loved in OpenMusic — ロストライン (茉ひる · Billy Choi) — using lyric repeat and the ±0.5s lyrics timer to hear a single line head-to-tail, over and over. In about 10 minutes, one line went from unreadable to singable, including three kanji readings he had never known. His verdict: real, loved material beats simple circling tasks — one song, one line at a time.

The core loop: search a song through OpenMusic, split it line by line, tap any word for its reading and meaning, hear the same sound across many voices and versions, and sing the line back. Scaffolding (romaji, furigana) fades automatically as recall becomes real. A science-based memory engine (FSRS-class, per item) reheats each line just before it would be forgotten — reheat, don't re-cook.

And the gate is your voice: in the Dictation Gate (2026-11-21), the learner always answers by voice, and passing means a native-grade dictation engine can transcribe you correctly, again and again. Fail = drill again, privately, infinitely patient. Pass = recorded, celebrated, next level.

OpenLanguage 唔係坐喺度諗出嚟 — 係先活過，先成立。2026-11-19 通宵嗰晚，創辦人喺 OpenMusic loop 一首一聽鍾情嘅日文歌 — ロストライン（茉ひる · Billy Choi）— 用歌詞重播同 ±0.5 秒歌詞計時，將一行歌詞由頭到尾聽完又聽。大約 10 分鐘，一行由睇唔明變到唱得出，包括三個從未識嘅漢字讀音。佢嘅判語：真材實料、自己鍾意嘅歌，勝過原地打圈嘅簡單任務 — 一首歌，一行一行嚟。

核心循環：透過 OpenMusic 搵歌，逐行拆開，撳任何一隻字睇讀音同意思，同一個音聽勻多把聲、多個版本，再唱返出嚟。輔助（羅馬字、振假名）會隨住真識咗而自動淡出。科學記憶引擎（FSRS 級、逐項排程）喺你就嚟唔記得之前翻熱嗰行 — 翻熱，唔係重新煮。

而把關嘅係你把聲：聽寫閘（Dictation Gate，2026-11-21）— 學員永遠用聲答，過關 = 原生級聽寫引擎可以穩定正確咁寫出你講嘅嘢。唔過 = 私下再練，無限耐性；過咗 = 記錄、慶祝、下一關。

END GOAL最終目標

Sight → sound, as a reflex. See a character, and the sound fires — physiologically, with no intermediate step, like a native. The sole purpose recorded at founding: teach more people more foreign languages — not their mother tongue — through songs they chose themselves. The ceiling is not one language pair: it is every language on Earth.

見字 → 出聲，成為反射。見到一隻字，個音即刻彈出嚟 — 生理級、冇中間步，好似母語者一樣。創立時記錄嘅唯一目的：透過學習者自己揀嘅歌，教更多人學更多外語（唔係母語）。天花板唔係一對語言 — 係地球上所有語言。

ROADMAP路線圖

  1. Japanese first, deeply — architecture language-generic from day one; content and dogfooding launch with Japanese (hiragana + katakana to mother-tongue ease, per-kana diagnosis).
  2. The core loop — OpenMusic search → line-by-line split → tap-a-word readings → contrast listening across voices → sing-back, with the microphone in from day one.
  3. The memory engine — FSRS-class per-item scheduling; idol-voiced forced-recall reviews at the forgetting edge.
  4. The Dictation Gate — voice answers certified by render stability across repetitions.
  5. Every language on Earth — any source → target pair, self-selected songs, era-spanning voices.
  1. 日文先行，做深 — 架構第一日就語言通用；內容同 dogfood 先由日文出發（平假名＋片假名去到母語級流暢，逐個假名診斷）。
  2. 核心循環 — OpenMusic 搵歌 → 逐行拆 → 撳字睇讀音 → 多把聲對比聆聽 → 唱返出嚟，咪高峰第一日就有。
  3. 記憶引擎 — FSRS 級逐項排程；喺遺忘邊緣用原唱把聲做強制回憶複習。
  4. 聽寫閘 — 用聲作答，以重複渲染穩定度認證過關。
  5. 地球上所有語言 — 任意來源 → 目標語言配對、自選歌曲、跨年代聲音。
